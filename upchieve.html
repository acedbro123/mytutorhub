⚡ What Is UpChieve?

UpChieve is an on-demand tutoring platform where volunteers help low-income high school students. Volunteers log in whenever they're free and get instantly matched with a student who needs help right now — no scheduling required. Sessions use text chat and a shared whiteboard to cover STEM subjects, SAT prep, and essay review.

👥 Who Is It For?

📐

STEM Volunteers

Strong in math, science, or physics? UpChieve has constant demand for tutors in these subjects.

✍️

Writing Coaches

Confident writers can help students with essay writing, SAT reading, and college application prep.

⏱️

Flexible Schedulers

No set commitment — volunteer for an hour here and there whenever your schedule allows.

🌱

First-Time Volunteers

No experience needed. UpChieve's simple drop-in format makes it a great starting point for new tutors.

⚖️ Opportunities & Considerations

✅ Opportunities

  • Volunteer entirely on your own schedule — no appointments to keep
  • High-impact work: your help reaches students with no other tutoring options
  • No minimum hour commitment — do as much or as little as you want
  • Platform is easy to use — text chat and whiteboard, no fancy setup
  • No need to prep a lesson plan — just jump in and help with whatever the student brings
  • Volunteer hours are tracked automatically on your profile

Considerations

  • You may wait a while after going "online" before a student requests help
  • It can be difficult to explain complex math problems on the platform
  • No ongoing relationship — you'll work with a different student each time

🚀 How to Get Started

1

Apply online

Fill out the volunteer application on the UpChieve website. You'll provide basic info and select which subjects you're comfortable coaching.

2

Complete onboarding

UpChieve has a short training and onboarding quiz to ensure volunteers understand how sessions work and the platform's approach to helping students.

3

Go online and wait for a match

Log into the platform, click "Go Online," and the system will notify you when a student requests help in your subject area. You'll be matched automatically.

4

Help the student

Use the chat and whiteboard tools to work through the student's problem. Most sessions are 30–60 minutes. After the session, give brief feedback through the platform.
