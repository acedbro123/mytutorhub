📘 What Is Schoolhouse.world?

Schoolhouse.world is a peer-to-peer tutoring platform founded by Sal Khan of Khan Academy where volunteers tutor students live via video with a shared whiteboard, covering everything from pre-algebra to AP courses and SAT prep. Tutors build public profiles, earn subject certifications, and are widely recognized by colleges for their contributions.

👥 Who Is It For?

🧑‍🎓

Tutors (Volunteers)

High school and college students who are strong in a subject and want to help others.

📚

Learners (Students)

K–12 and college students who need help with academic subjects — from math to SAT prep.

🌍

Global Learners

Open to students from all over the world. Sessions are conducted in English.

📝

Test Preppers

Students preparing for the SAT, ACT, or AP exams benefit greatly from Schoolhouse tutors.

⚖️ Opportunities & Considerations

Opportunities

  • Tutor students from around the world without leaving your home
  • Wide variety of subjects — you choose what you're confident in
  • Build a public profile and earn verifiable tutor certifications
  • Strong, supportive peer community with active Discord and events
  • Volunteer hours are logged automatically on your profile
  • Group sessions mean you help multiple students at once

Considerations

  • You must pass a subject certification quiz before tutoring in that area
  • Group sessions can sometimes be harder to manage than 1-on-1
  • You're responsible for planning and running each session yourself

🚀 How to Get Started

1

Create a free account

Go to schoolhouse.world and sign up with your email or Google account. It only takes a minute.

2

Take a certification quiz

Choose the subject you'd like to tutor and complete a short mastery quiz. This ensures tutors genuinely know their content. You can retake the quiz if needed.

3

Set up your tutor profile

Add a bio, subjects you teach, and your weekly availability. A strong profile helps students find and trust you.

4

Schedule your first session

Create a tutoring session on the platform. Learners will sign up and receive a calendar invite. Sessions are held right on the Schoolhouse platform via video + whiteboard.

5

Download your volunteer hours

After sessions, your volunteer hours are automatically tracked. You can export a record from your profile anytime for school requirements.
